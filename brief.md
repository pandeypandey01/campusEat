# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system that allows students to discover food options on campus, place orders, and track their orders. Food-service staff can manage available items and process incoming orders.

The system provides a simple way to connect students who want food with campus food providers who prepare and fulfill those orders.

## Who

- **Students:** Browse food items, place orders, and track order status.
- **Food-service staff:** Manage food items, receive orders, and update order status.
- **Campus administrators:** Manage the overall system, users, and food-service information.

## Summary

CampusEats connects students with campus food providers through an online ordering workflow. Students can find food, create and pay for orders, and monitor their status. Food providers can manage menus and process orders, while administrators maintain the system and its users.
